Phishing Investigation Workflow

Suspicious Email
      ↓
Examine Sender
      ↓
Check Email Content
      ↓
Inspect Links / Attachments
      ↓
Identify Indicators
      ↓
Determine Malicious Activity
      ↓
Report / Respond

What I Learned

I learned how to examine suspicious emails and identify common indicators of phishing.

I also learned that attackers can use social engineering techniques to make malicious emails appear legitimate and encourage users to click links, open attachments, or provide sensitive information.

SOC Relevance

Phishing alerts are commonly investigated by SOC teams. Understanding email indicators and analyzing suspicious messages helps analysts identify potential threats and protect users and organizations.
