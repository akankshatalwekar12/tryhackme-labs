What I Learned

I learned how a SOC L1 Analyst performs the initial investigation of security alerts.

The main focus is to understand what happened, examine the available evidence, determine whether the alert requires further investigation, and escalate or respond appropriately.

SOC Relevance

Alert triage is an important part of a SOC Analyst's daily work. It helps analysts prioritize security events and investigate potentially malicious activity efficiently.
